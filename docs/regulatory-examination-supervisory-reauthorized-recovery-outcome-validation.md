# Supervisory Reauthorized Recovery Outcome Validation, Enterprise Recovery Recertification & Sustainability Reclosure Assurance

This capability validates the outcome of the supervisory reauthorized recovery execution established in the preceding recovery-governance layer. It requires immutable Release 92 execution provenance and keeps recovery certification, residual-risk acceptance, commitment closure, recertification and program reclosure exclusively human-controlled.

## Outcome validation

The service reconciles evidence-bound workstream completion, cross-entity control re-transformation, repeated-failure control effectiveness, systemic-risk reduction, regulatory commitment implementation, blockers, and sustainability observations. Re-transformed controls are not treated as effective merely because implementation completed; repeated-failure controls require independent testing and evidence provenance tied to the Release 92 execution version.

## Independent assurance and human authority

Independent reviewers validate the recovery outcome against Release 92 execution scope and independent assurance evidence. AI, RAG, agents, MCP services and workers may calculate scores, detect blockers or degradation, and recommend additional review, but they cannot certify recovery effectiveness or accept residual systemic risk.

Executive recertification requires references to the Release 92 supervisory execution, Release 92 independent assurance, independent outcome validation, the human residual-risk decision and the sustainability assessment. Sustainability reclosure additionally requires an immutable human recovery-recertification reference.

## Reclosure readiness gates

Reclosure readiness requires Release 92 execution and assurance provenance, completed supervisory recovery outcomes, reconciled cross-entity re-transformation, effective repeated-failure controls, independent outcome validation, verified systemic-risk reduction, cleared blockers, reconciled regulatory commitments, a passed sustainability window and a recorded human residual-risk decision.

Traceability is preserved as:

`Release 91 supervisory reauthorization -> Release 92 supervisory execution -> execution evidence -> independent outcome validation -> human residual-risk reassessment -> executive recovery recertification -> sustainability reclosure`
