# Regulatory Examination Reopened-Commitment Remediation, Supervisory Revalidation & Reclosure Assurance

This capability governs a commitment after a human-authorized reopening. It creates a renewed remediation plan, renewed milestones and dependencies, propagates corrective action across affected entities, links regulator follow-up items, compares prior and current root causes, supports recommendation-only control redesign analysis, captures independent human retesting, detects second recurrence, defines a sustainability reset window, and requires authorized human recertification before reclosure.

## Non-delegable authority

AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers may analyze, retrieve, compare, recommend and monitor. They may not recertify or reclose a commitment, accept residual risk, represent regulator intent, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.

## Reclosure gates

A reclosure readiness score reaches 100 only when the renewed plan is approved, all milestones are complete, cross-entity propagation is complete, regulator follow-up is reconciled, independent retesting passes, independent revalidation is complete, evidence is sufficient, the sustainability reset is ready, and no second recurrence is unresolved.

## Traceability

`reopened commitment -> renewed remediation -> evidence -> independent retest -> human recertification -> sustainability reset -> reclosure`

All recertification and reclosure decisions are immutable, tenant-scoped, actor-attributed and SHA-256 version hashed.
