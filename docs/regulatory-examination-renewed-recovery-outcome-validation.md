# Regulatory Examination Renewed Recovery Outcome Validation, Enterprise Recovery Recertification & Sustainability Reclosure Assurance

This capability validates the outcome of a human-authorized renewed recovery program after execution and independent revalidation. It measures systemic-risk reduction, reconciles cross-entity rehabilitation completion, validates independent recovery evidence, reconciles regulatory commitments, evaluates sustainability windows and prepares an evidence-bound reclosure-readiness record.

## Governance sequence

`renewed recovery execution -> evidence -> independent validation -> human residual-risk reassessment -> executive recovery recertification -> human sustainability reclosure`

## Non-delegable authority

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers are limited to analysis, recommendation and monitoring. They cannot accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, recertify recovery, reclose intervention programs, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

Independent recovery validation must be performed by an authorized human assurance role. Residual-risk acceptance/rejection, recovery recertification and program reclosure require authorized executive human decisions and immutable version references.

## Production controls

- Tenant-scoped API/service boundaries.
- Deterministic SHA-256 version hashes for assurance and certification records.
- Baseline-to-current systemic-risk reduction measurement.
- Cross-entity completion and evidence reconciliation.
- Independent recovery-effectiveness validation.
- Regulatory commitment completion reconciliation without automated closure.
- Sustainability observation-window and control-health stabilization checks.
- Explicit unresolved-blocker gates.
- Human-only residual-risk decisions, recertification and reclosure.
- Monitoring-only worker alerts suitable for SSE supervisory dashboards.
- Immutable audit trail for outcome, assurance, certification and reclosure decisions.
