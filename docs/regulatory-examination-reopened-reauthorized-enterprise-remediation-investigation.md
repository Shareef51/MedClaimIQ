# Reopened Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Investigation, Systemic Remediation Failure Root-Cause Reconstruction and Enterprise Remediation Reauthorization

This capability begins only after the Release 102 surveillance layer records an immutable **human enterprise reopening decision**. AI reopening candidates, surveillance scores, correlation signals, or inferred regulatory concern never constitute authority to open this investigation.

## Multi-cycle remediation evidence reconstruction

The investigation reconstructs the remediation history across prior human reauthorization, execution, root-cause treatments, systemic control re-transformations, independent effectiveness assurance, recovery outcome validation, executive recertification, sustainability reclosure, post-reclosure surveillance and the Release 102 reopening. Missing cycle evidence remains an explicit investigation blocker.

## Persistent and emergent treatment failure

Root-cause treatments are assessed separately from control failures. The platform identifies persistent causes whose treatments failed or regressed and emergent causes that appeared after the prior remediation cycle. These results are analytic candidates only; authoritative root-cause confirmation is human-controlled.

## Systemic remediation-failure root-cause reconstruction

The analysis combines treatment failure, repeated systemic control failure, repeated remediation cycles, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity recurrence and material regulatory commitment breaches. It may propose persistent or emergent systemic root-cause candidates, but cannot make the authoritative conclusion.

## Prior recertification and reclosure assumptions

Release 101 executive recertification/reclosure assumptions are re-tested against current evidence. Contradicted, stale, unsupported, expired or treatment-invalidated assumptions are surfaced for human review.

## Cross-entity causality and regulatory impact

High-confidence causal propagation links are analyzed across entities, controls, root causes and root-cause treatments. Regulatory commitments and follow-ups are correlated to the reopened remediation failure. Human interpretation is mandatory, and regulator intent is never inferred or represented by the platform.

## Systemic remediation-failure classification

The system may propose `localized`, `material`, `enterprise_systemic`, or `enterprise_critical`. The result is recommendation-only. Human confirmation of both the systemic root causes and the systemic remediation-failure classification is required.

## Enterprise remediation reauthorization

A renewed enterprise remediation strategy remains a candidate until the investigation is formally concluded. Reauthorization requires verified Release 102 human reopening provenance, complete multi-cycle evidence, human-validated persistent/emergent treatment failures, validated prior assumptions, human-confirmed systemic root causes, assessed repeated control failures, human-validated cross-entity causality, human-interpreted regulatory impacts, human-confirmed systemic remediation-failure classification, a documented renewed strategy, independent internal-audit challenge by a segregated actor, executive review and a complete evidence-bound package.

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers cannot open authoritative investigations, confirm systemic root causes, confirm systemic remediation-failure classification, authorize remediation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose programs, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability

`Release 102 human enterprise reopening -> formal remediation investigation -> multi-cycle remediation evidence -> systemic remediation-failure root-cause reconstruction -> human confirmation -> systemic remediation-failure classification -> human confirmation -> renewed remediation strategy -> independent challenge -> executive human remediation reauthorization`
