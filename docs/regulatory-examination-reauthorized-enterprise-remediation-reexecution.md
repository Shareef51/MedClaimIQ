# Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Re-Execution, Systemic Control Re-Transformation & Independent Recovery Effectiveness Assurance

This capability begins only after an immutable **Release 103 human enterprise remediation reauthorization** with decision `authorize`. A Release 102 reopening, investigation analysis, AI recommendation, systemic-remediation-failure score, or strategy candidate is never sufficient execution authority.

## Re-execution provenance
Every remediation re-execution program preserves the Release 103 recovery-program reference, investigation and conclusion versions, human root-cause confirmation, human systemic-remediation-failure classification, renewed strategy, independent challenge, and executive reauthorization version.

## Persistent and emergent treatment workstreams
Human-confirmed persistent and emergent systemic causes are mapped to owned workstreams, transformed controls, affected entities, regulatory commitments and evidence. Missing Release 103 scope, ownership, evidence or human treatment confirmation blocks readiness.

## Systemic control re-transformation
Repeated-failure controls may be replaced, retired/redesigned or re-transformed. Each authoritative transformation requires a separate immutable human approval tied to the Release 103 reauthorization scope and root-cause treatment.

## Cross-entity sequencing, critical path and drift
The platform detects sequencing collisions, unmet dependencies, stale evidence, blocked critical milestones, implementation drift and KPI underperformance. It never silently changes rollout order or approves remediation.

## Independent recovery-effectiveness assurance
Independent reviewers validate the reauthorized scope, systemic root-cause treatment, repeated-failure coverage, cross-entity effectiveness and evidence completeness. Segregation of duties prevents an implementation owner from independently assuring their own work. Passing assurance is evidence for a later human outcome/certification process; it is not certification.

## Authority boundary
AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers can plan, analyze, recommend, detect drift, score KPIs and monitor. They cannot authorize remediation re-execution, approve systemic control re-transformation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close commitments, reclose the program, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability
`Release 103 human enterprise remediation reauthorization -> remediation re-execution -> persistent/emergent root-cause treatment -> systemic control re-transformation -> cross-entity implementation evidence -> independent recovery effectiveness assurance -> enterprise-wide validation -> human outcome review`
