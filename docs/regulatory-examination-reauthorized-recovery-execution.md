# Reauthorized Recovery Execution and Independent Recovery Assurance

This capability executes a human-reauthorized recovery strategy after repeated recovery failure. It governs enterprise control re-rehabilitation, repeated-failure control replacement, cross-entity sequencing, regulatory commitment alignment, evidence-bound execution checkpoints, critical paths, drift, recovery KPIs, and independent recovery assurance.

## Authority boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may analyze, recommend, monitor, score and orchestrate bounded workflow steps. They may not approve control re-rehabilitation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reclose a program, represent regulator intent, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

A human remediation reauthorization version from the repeated-recovery-failure investigation is mandatory before authoritative execution begins. Independent assurance is evidence-producing only; final certification remains human authority.

## Traceability

`human remediation reauthorization -> reauthorized recovery execution -> evidence -> independent recovery assurance`
