# Reclosed Recovery Sustainability Monitoring

This capability monitors reclosed enterprise recovery programs across multiple examination/remediation cycles. It detects control-health decay, systemic-risk rebound, repeated recovery failure, cross-entity recurrence propagation and regulator-follow-up pressure.

## Governance boundary
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers are recommendation/monitoring-only. They may calculate recurrence/materiality signals but cannot open an authoritative investigation, reopen/reclose a program, accept residual risk or certify recovery effectiveness. Repeated recovery failure requires an independent human investigation plus executive/internal-audit challenge.

## Traceability
`reclosed recovery -> sustainability surveillance -> multi-cycle recurrence -> enterprise escalation -> human investigation`
