# Supervisory Reauthorized Recovery Execution, Enterprise Control Re-Transformation & Independent Recovery Assurance

This capability executes the human-authorized recovery strategy produced by the reopened repeated-failure investigation. It requires immutable Release 91 supervisory recovery reauthorization and investigation references before authoritative execution can begin.

## Governed execution

The layer governs supervisory recovery workstreams, repeated-failure corrective actions, enterprise control re-transformation, repeated-failure control replacement, cross-entity deployment sequencing, regulatory commitment alignment, evidence-bound execution checkpoints, dependency and critical-path review, implementation drift, recovery KPI baselines, independent retesting, cross-entity effectiveness assurance, unresolved blockers, and executive progress decisions.

## Authority boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may plan, analyze, recommend, score, detect drift and monitor bounded execution. They may not approve control re-transformation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reclose a program, represent regulator intent, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

Independent assurance is evidence-producing and must be performed by an authorized independent human reviewer. It does not itself certify recovery effectiveness. Executive progress decisions remain human decisions.

## Traceability

`Release 91 human supervisory recovery reauthorization -> supervisory reauthorized recovery execution -> control re-transformation -> execution evidence -> independent recovery assurance -> human outcome review`
