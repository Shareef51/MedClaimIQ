# Renewed Recovery Program Execution and Independent Recovery Revalidation

This capability executes a **human-authorized** renewed recovery strategy after systemic recovery decay has been investigated and remediation has been explicitly authorized.

It governs renewed recovery workstreams, cross-entity control rehabilitation, failed-control replacement, implementation milestones, regulatory commitment mapping, dependencies and critical paths, evidence-bound checkpoints, implementation drift, recovery KPIs, independent recovery retesting and executive progress review.

## Non-delegable authority

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may analyze, recommend, monitor and orchestrate bounded work. They may not approve control rehabilitation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reclose intervention programs, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

Independent recovery revalidation must be performed by an authorized human independent-assurance role. Program and control decisions remain human-controlled and are stored as immutable versions with deterministic SHA-256 hashes.

## Traceability

`human authorization -> renewed recovery execution -> evidence -> independent revalidation -> recovery outcome`
