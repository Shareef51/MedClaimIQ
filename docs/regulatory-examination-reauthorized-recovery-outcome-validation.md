# Reauthorized Recovery Outcome Validation and Sustainability Reclosure Governance

This capability validates the outcome of a human-reauthorized recovery program after repeated recovery failure. It reconciles cross-entity re-rehabilitation completion, validates repeated-failure controls, measures systemic-risk reduction, performs independent outcome assurance, reconciles regulatory commitments, evaluates sustainability/control-health stabilization, and produces deterministic readiness for human executive recertification and reclosure.

## Authority boundary
AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers are analysis/recommendation/monitoring only. They cannot accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, recertify recovery, or reclose the program. Those decisions require authorized human actors and immutable evidence/version references.

## Traceability
`human reauthorization -> reauthorized recovery execution -> evidence -> independent outcome validation -> human residual-risk reassessment -> executive recovery recertification -> sustainability reclosure`
