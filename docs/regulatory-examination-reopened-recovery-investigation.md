# Regulatory Examination Reopened Recovery Investigation and Renewed Remediation Authorization

This capability governs reopened-recovery investigations after post-reclosure surveillance identifies systemic recovery decay. It reconstructs decay evidence across cycles, validates prior recovery assumptions, reassesses root causes, maps cross-entity control gaps, correlates regulator follow-ups and commitments, and prepares evidence-bound renewed remediation authorization packages.

## Non-delegable authority
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers are recommendation/analysis/monitoring-only. They cannot authorize renewed remediation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, reopen/reclose programs, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.

## Traceability
`reopened recovery -> systemic investigation -> root-cause reassessment -> renewed remediation strategy -> human authorization -> governed recovery execution`

Independent challenge and executive human authorization are mandatory before renewed remediation execution may proceed.
