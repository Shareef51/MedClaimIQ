# Regulatory Examination Reclosed Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Re-Execution Surveillance

This capability governs post-reclosure surveillance after the enterprise has completed reauthorized remediation re-execution, independent effectiveness assurance, outcome validation, executive recovery recertification, and human sustainability reclosure.

## Purpose

The layer continuously evaluates whether the re-executed remediation remains sustainable. It detects persistent/emergent root-cause treatment decay, systemic control re-transformation regression, repeated-failure control recurrence, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity propagation, stale evidence, sustainability breaches, regulatory-commitment breaches, new examination/finding correlation, and adverse regulator follow-ups.

Every surveillance and analysis request is bound to the exact Release 105 human sustainability-reclosure version. The lineage therefore remains traceable to the Release 104 remediation re-execution and Release 103 human remediation reauthorization represented by that reclosure package.

## Governance boundary

AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools, and workers may monitor, score, correlate, summarize, and prepare reopening candidates. They may not open an authoritative investigation, confirm root causes, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery, close a regulatory commitment, infer regulator intent, reopen or reclose the program, mutate accounting records, authorize payment, collect funds, or move funds.

Authoritative reopening requires a human-created investigation, evidence-bound independent reassessment performed under segregation of duties, executive/internal-audit challenge, human validation of materiality and linked evidence, deterministic reopening-readiness gates, and a separate authorized executive reopening decision.

## Deterministic reopening gates

Reopening readiness requires validated Release 105 sustainability-reclosure provenance, confirmed material systemic recovery decay, human-validated root-cause treatment decay, completed human investigation, completed independent reassessment, prior executive recertification/reclosure comparison, validated cross-entity recurrence scope, human-validated examination/finding links, human-interpreted regulator follow-ups, human-validated enterprise materiality, completed executive review, completed internal-audit challenge, and a prepared renewed enterprise recovery governance candidate.

## Persistence and auditability

The migration creates separate immutable record stores for surveillance versions, human decay investigations, independent reassessments, executive/internal-audit challenges, and enterprise reopening decisions. Dashboard and audit-export surfaces preserve deterministic hashes and evidence references for supervisory review.
