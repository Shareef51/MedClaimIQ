# Regulatory Examination Reclosed Recovery Surveillance

This capability continuously monitors human-reclosed enterprise recovery programs for systemic-risk rebound, recovery-effectiveness decay, cross-entity regression, new examination/finding matches, sustainability breaches, and adverse regulator follow-up signals.

## Governance boundary

AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers are monitoring/recommendation-only. They may detect and correlate signals or create a reopening candidate, but cannot reopen or reclose a program, accept residual systemic risk, or certify recovery effectiveness. Reopening requires a completed human investigation, independent reassessment, executive review, internal-audit review, comparison to the prior certification, and a renewed remediation candidate.

## Traceability

`reclosed recovery -> surveillance signal -> systemic decay -> human investigation -> independent reassessment -> executive/internal-audit review -> human enterprise reopening -> renewed remediation`

All surveillance, investigation, reassessment and reopening decisions are immutable, tenant-scoped and hash-versioned for audit reconstruction.
