# Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Outcome Validation, Systemic Recovery Recertification & Sustainability Reclosure Governance

This capability validates the outcome of the enterprise reauthorized recovery execution produced by the preceding governance layer. It requires immutable Release 100 enterprise-execution and independent-effectiveness-assurance provenance and keeps residual-risk acceptance, recovery certification, regulatory commitment closure, executive recertification, and sustainability reclosure exclusively human-controlled.

## Enterprise outcome validation

The service reconciles evidence-bound workstream completion, human-approved systemic control re-transformation, enterprise-wide completion, repeated-failure control effectiveness, systemic-risk reduction, regulatory commitment implementation, unresolved blockers, cross-entity control health, and sustainability observations. Implementation completion alone is never treated as proof of control effectiveness.

Repeated-failure controls require independent tests bound to both Release 100 execution and Release 100 independent-assurance references. Cross-entity control-health stabilization requires evidence and execution provenance for every in-scope entity.

## Independent assurance and segregation of duties

Independent outcome assurance is restricted to independent human assurance roles. An implementation owner cannot independently validate their own work. AI, RAG, agents, MCP services and workers may calculate scores, reconcile evidence, detect degradation, and recommend additional review but cannot issue authoritative effectiveness certification.

## Human residual risk, recertification and reclosure

Human residual-systemic-risk reassessment requires Release 100 execution, Release 100 independent assurance, Release 101 independent outcome validation, sustainability assessment, and evidence references. Executive recertification requires explicit human residual-risk acceptance and deterministic reclosure readiness. Sustainability reclosure is a separate human decision and additionally requires a passed sustainability assurance state.

## Sustainability reclosure readiness

Readiness requires Release 100 execution/assurance provenance, complete enterprise outcomes, reconciled control re-transformation, independently effective repeated-failure controls, independent enterprise outcome validation, verified systemic-risk reduction, reconciled commitments, cleared blockers, stabilized cross-entity control health, a passed 180-day sustainability window, and a recorded human residual-risk decision.

Traceability is preserved as:

`Release 99 human enterprise recovery reauthorization -> Release 100 enterprise execution -> implementation evidence -> independent effectiveness assurance -> Release 101 independent outcome validation -> human residual-risk reassessment -> executive systemic recovery recertification -> sustainability reclosure`


## Root-cause treatment effectiveness

Persistent and emergent root-cause treatments are reconciled against the Release 99 human-confirmed root-cause scope and the Release 100 remediation execution evidence. Treatment completion does not equal effectiveness; evidence and outcome validation are required before recertification.
