# Reopened Supervisory Recovery Investigation, Multi-Cycle Root-Cause Reconstruction and Enterprise Recovery Reauthorization

This capability begins only after the reclosed supervisory recovery surveillance layer records an immutable **human enterprise reopening decision**. A monitoring signal, AI reopening candidate or inferred supervisory concern is not sufficient authority to begin the authoritative investigation.

## Formal multi-cycle investigation

The investigation reconstructs the complete recovery history across reauthorization, execution, independent assurance, executive recertification, sustainability reclosure, surveillance decay and reopening cycles. Evidence gaps are surfaced rather than silently inferred. Root-cause analysis separates persistent causes that survived prior remediation from newly emergent causes and records prior causes that no longer appear active.

## Recertification and reclosure assumption challenge

Prior executive recertification and reclosure assumptions are explicitly reassessed against new evidence. Breached, contradicted, stale, materially changed or unverified assumptions become governance blockers requiring human interpretation.

## Enterprise systemic-failure classification

AI may calculate a systemic-failure score and propose `localized`, `material`, `enterprise_systemic` or `enterprise_critical`. The proposal is not an authoritative classification. Human confirmation is required, as is human confirmation of persistent/emergent root causes. Cross-entity causal propagation and regulator follow-up impacts likewise require human validation or interpretation.

## Reauthorization governance

A renewed recovery strategy is recommendation-only. Human enterprise recovery reauthorization requires Release 94 reopening provenance, a formal investigation conclusion, complete multi-cycle evidence reconstruction, human-confirmed root causes, human-confirmed systemic-failure classification, assessed control re-transformation failures, human-validated cross-entity causality, human-interpreted regulator follow-ups, independent internal-audit challenge, executive review and an evidence-bound authorization package.

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers cannot open authoritative investigations, confirm regulatory/systemic conclusions, authorize recovery remediation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose programs, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability

`human enterprise reopening -> reopened supervisory recovery investigation -> multi-cycle evidence reconstruction -> persistent/emergent root-cause reconstruction -> enterprise systemic-failure classification -> independent challenge -> renewed recovery strategy -> human enterprise recovery reauthorization`
