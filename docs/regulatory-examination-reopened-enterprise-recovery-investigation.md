# Reopened Enterprise Recovery Investigation, Systemic Recovery Failure Root-Cause Reconstruction and Enterprise Remediation Reauthorization

This capability begins only after the reclosed enterprise recovery surveillance layer records an immutable **Release 98 human enterprise reopening decision**. AI reopening candidates, surveillance alerts, systemic-decay scores or inferred regulator concerns are not sufficient authority to open the authoritative investigation.

## Full multi-cycle enterprise investigation

The investigation reconstructs the recovery history across authorization, execution, independent assurance, executive recertification, sustainability reclosure, post-reclosure surveillance and reopening cycles. Missing evidence remains an explicit blocker. Root-cause analysis separates persistent systemic causes that survived prior remediation from newly emergent causes.

## Prior assumption and systemic-control challenge

Prior executive recertification/reclosure assumptions are retested against new evidence. Repeated systemic control re-transformation failures are analyzed across entities and recovery cycles. Cross-entity causal links require human validation before they are treated as authoritative causality.

## Regulatory commitments and follow-ups

The platform correlates breached or overdue commitments and regulator follow-ups to the reopened recovery investigation. AI may identify correlations and materiality candidates, but human interpretation is mandatory and regulator intent is never inferred or represented by the system.

## Enterprise systemic-failure classification

The system calculates an evidence-derived score and may propose `localized`, `material`, `enterprise_systemic` or `enterprise_critical`. The proposal is not an authoritative conclusion. Human confirmation is required for both root causes and enterprise systemic-failure classification.

## Remediation reauthorization governance

A renewed enterprise remediation strategy is recommendation-only. Reauthorization requires Release 98 human reopening provenance, a formal investigation conclusion, complete multi-cycle evidence, human-confirmed root causes, human-confirmed systemic-failure classification, assessed repeated control failures, human-validated cross-entity causality, human-interpreted regulatory commitment/follow-up impacts, independent internal-audit challenge by a different actor, executive review and a complete evidence-bound authorization package.

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers cannot open authoritative investigations, confirm systemic conclusions, authorize enterprise remediation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose programs, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability

`human enterprise reopening -> reopened enterprise recovery investigation -> multi-cycle evidence reconstruction -> systemic root-cause reconstruction -> enterprise systemic-failure classification -> renewed remediation strategy -> independent challenge -> human enterprise remediation reauthorization`
