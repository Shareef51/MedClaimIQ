# Regulatory Examination Commitment Lifecycle

This capability extends confirmed examination commitments into a governed lifecycle from owner assignment through human-certified completion and regulator follow-up.

## Traceability

`confirmed commitment -> accountable owner -> milestones/dependencies -> versioned evidence -> effectiveness validation -> human completion certification -> regulator follow-up`

## Controls

- Confirmed commitments are immutable/versioned records; amendments create governed revisions rather than silent overwrites.
- Milestones have owners, due dates, dependencies, evidence requirements and supervisory risk state.
- Written and verbal records are reconciled; discrepancies require human review.
- Completion requires all required milestones, provenance-bound evidence and human effectiveness validation.
- Only authorized humans can certify commitment completion or approve amendments.
- AI, RAG, LangGraph agents, MCP tools and workers are monitoring/recommendation-only and cannot change obligations or communicate autonomously with regulators.
- Tenant isolation, audit logging, SHA-256 evidence provenance and SSE risk events are required production controls.

## Supervisory events

`regulatory.commitment_lifecycle.commitment_at_risk`, `milestone_at_risk`, and `follow_up_at_risk` are generated by the monitoring worker for human action.
