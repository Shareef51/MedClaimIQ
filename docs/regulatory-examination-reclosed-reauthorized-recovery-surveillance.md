# Reclosed Reauthorized Recovery Surveillance and Enterprise Reopening Governance

This capability supervises recovery programs after a human-approved reauthorization, execution, independent outcome validation, executive recertification, and sustainability reclosure. It is designed for repeated-failure conditions where prior recovery cycles may have appeared effective but later decay or recur.

## Surveillance model

The runtime monitors repeated-failure control regression, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity recurrence propagation, deterioration relative to the prior recertification/reclosure baseline, newly correlated examination findings, and regulator follow-up signals. Correlations are evidence-supporting signals only; they never infer or represent regulator intent.

## Human governance boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may score, correlate, summarize, and recommend. They cannot open authoritative investigations, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, reopen/reclose enterprise programs, close regulatory commitments, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.

A program reopening requires a human supervisory investigation, an independent reassessment, executive and internal-audit challenge, validation of prior recertification/reclosure comparisons and cross-entity scope, human interpretation of examination/follow-up links, and a renewed recovery-governance candidate. The final reopening decision is human-only and immutable.

## Traceability

`reauthorized recovery reclosure -> surveillance -> repeated decay -> human investigation -> independent reassessment -> executive/internal-audit challenge -> human reopening -> renewed recovery governance`
