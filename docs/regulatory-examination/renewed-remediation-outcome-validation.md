# Renewed Remediation Outcome Validation, Recovery Certification & Sustainability Reclosure Governance

This capability validates outcomes from human-authorized renewed enterprise remediation without delegating regulatory or executive authority to AI.

## Controls
- Baseline vs post-remediation systemic-risk measurement.
- Independent recovery validation and cross-entity reconciliation.
- Regulatory commitment completion reconciliation and blocker governance.
- Sustainability observation windows before reclosure.
- Human-only residual systemic-risk acceptance.
- Human-only executive recovery certification and intervention-program reclosure.
- Immutable version hashes for assurance, risk-acceptance, certification, observation, and reclosure records.

## Authority boundary
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers may analyze, summarize, monitor, score and recommend. They cannot accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, reclose intervention programs, close regulator commitments, modify accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.
