# Reclosed Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Surveillance, Systemic Recovery Decay Intelligence and Enterprise Reopening Governance

This capability begins only after the Release 101 human residual-systemic-risk reassessment, executive systemic recovery recertification and separate human sustainability reclosure. It continuously evaluates whether the reauthorized enterprise remediation remains sustainable without converting AI or worker surveillance signals into authoritative regulatory, audit or executive decisions.

## Release 101 provenance

All authoritative investigation and reopening workflows require an immutable `release101_reauthorized_enterprise_remediation_sustainability_reclosure_version_id`. A model score, monitoring event or reopening candidate is never sufficient authority.

## Decay and recurrence intelligence

The runtime detects persistent/emergent root-cause treatment decay, systemic control re-transformation regression, multi-cycle recovery decay, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity recurrence propagation, stale evidence, adverse control tests and regulatory-commitment breaches. It also compares the current state with the Release 101 executive recertification/reclosure baseline and correlates new examination findings and regulator follow-ups without inferring regulator intent.

Enterprise materiality combines decay, root-cause treatment deterioration, control regression, recurrence, risk rebound, repeated recovery cycles and regulatory signals. Materiality is advisory until human validated.

## Human governance boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may monitor, score, correlate, summarize and prepare candidates. They cannot open authoritative investigations, determine regulator intent, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose the enterprise recovery program, modify accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

An authoritative investigation requires human authority plus evidence-bound surveillance and enterprise-materiality references. Reopening additionally requires independent reassessment with segregation of duties, prior executive recertification/reclosure comparison, validated root-cause-treatment decay and cross-entity recurrence scope, human validation of new examination links and materiality, human interpretation of regulator follow-ups, executive review, internal-audit challenge, and a renewed enterprise remediation-governance candidate.

## Traceability

`Release 101 human sustainability reclosure -> post-reclosure surveillance -> root-cause treatment decay/systemic control regression -> systemic recovery decay -> human investigation -> independent reassessment -> executive/internal-audit challenge -> human enterprise reopening -> renewed enterprise remediation governance`
