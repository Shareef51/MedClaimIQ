# Executive Certification, Enterprise Closure & Sustainability Assurance

Release 60 adds governed closure packages, deterministic closure-readiness gates, independent validation evidence, human residual-risk acceptance references, executive human certification, post-remediation sustainability windows, recurrence surveillance, reopen candidates, human reopen decisions, and regulator/audit traceability.

## Non-delegable authority
AI and workers may assess, summarize, draft, monitor, and escalate. They may not certify remediation, accept residual risk, close a finding or enterprise issue, sign an executive/board attestation, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.

## Closure gates
A closure package reaches 100 only when corrective-action evidence, retest evidence, independent validation, zero unresolved exceptions, validated compensating-control exit, and explicit human residual-risk acceptance are all present. Final certification also enforces segregation of duties between preparer and certifier.

## Sustainability
Closure is followed by a bounded assurance window. Insufficient observations or recurrence creates a reopen candidate; only an authorized human can decide to reopen, keep closed, or continue monitoring.

## Traceability
`deficiency -> corrective action -> retest -> independent validation -> human certification -> sustainability observations -> reopen decision / sustained closure`.
