# Reopened-Issue Outcome Validation & Recurrence Closure Assurance

Release 62 governs the supervisory lifecycle after a regulatory issue has been formally reopened. It validates whether renewed remediation actually fixes the recurring weakness, whether the fix propagates across affected entities, whether independent assurance supports the result, and whether the control remains sustainable before any human reclosure decision.

## Traceability

`reopened finding -> renewed remediation -> corrective action -> retest -> independent revalidation -> sustainability monitoring -> human recertification -> reclosure`

## Core controls

- Renewed remediation and corrective-action references are mandatory.
- Prior and current root causes are retained for comparative analysis.
- Control effectiveness is compared before and after renewed remediation.
- Recurrence-containment testing is explicit and evidence-linked.
- Independent revalidation evidence is mandatory.
- Cross-entity implementation must be complete where the recurring issue is systemic.
- Renewed regulatory commitments are tracked as reclosure gates.
- A configurable sustainability reset window must complete before reclosure readiness.
- A second recurrence automatically creates an escalation blocker.
- Reclosure readiness is deterministic, explainable, versioned, and advisory only.
- Final recertification and reclosure remain human-only decisions.

## Non-delegable authority

AI, agents, RAG, MCP tools and workers may analyze, retrieve, compare, score, monitor and recommend. They may not recertify controls, accept residual risk, reclose findings, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.
