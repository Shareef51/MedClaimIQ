# Regulatory Deficiency Lifecycle and Material Weakness Governance

Release 59 extends assurance exceptions and deficiency aggregation into a governed enterprise lifecycle. It provides deficiency investigations, formal human severity/classification decisions, cross-control impact and root-cause linkage, corrective-action plans and milestones, regulatory-commitment links, compensating-control expiry monitoring, recurring-deficiency and overdue-action surveillance, independent challenge, executive attestation, and immutable disposition history.

## Authority boundary
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers may analyze, prioritize, correlate, draft and monitor. They may not formally declare a material weakness/significant deficiency, approve corrective actions, accept residual risk, certify control effectiveness, close enterprise issues, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability
`exception -> deficiency -> enterprise issue -> human classification -> corrective action -> retest -> independent validation -> executive closure`

Formal significant-deficiency/material-weakness classifications require independent human challenge evidence. Executive closure requires both retest evidence and independent validation references. Segregation of duties prevents a corrective-action owner from approving their own plan.
