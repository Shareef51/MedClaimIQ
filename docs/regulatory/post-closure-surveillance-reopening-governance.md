# Release 61 — Post-Closure Surveillance, Recurrence Intelligence & Supervisory Reopening Governance

Release 61 extends Release 60 closure governance with long-term surveillance of certified-closed deficiencies and findings. It correlates new examinations, control-regression observations, sustainability decay, regulator follow-ups and cross-entity recurrence signals against prior closure evidence and certifications.

## Runtime chain
`closed issue -> surveillance signal -> recurrence evidence -> reopen candidate -> human reopening decision -> renewed corrective action -> independent revalidation`

## Guardrails
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers may retrieve, correlate, score, summarize and monitor only. They cannot reopen or close a finding, accept residual risk, certify control effectiveness, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Key controls
- tenant-scoped persistence and APIs
- immutable versioned reopen candidates
- prior-certification comparison references
- cross-entity recurrence propagation
- regulator follow-up references
- evidence-required human reopening
- renewed corrective-action linkage
- mandatory independent revalidation after reopening
- monitoring-only background worker
