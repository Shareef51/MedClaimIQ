# Enterprise Intervention Sustainability and Program Closure Governance

This capability governs the period after enterprise intervention execution. It measures systemic-risk reduction against a documented baseline, reconciles completion across entities and regulatory commitments, requires independent sustainability assurance, and blocks closure when evidence, commitments, dependencies, or sustainability windows are incomplete.

## Authority boundary
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools, and workers may calculate risk reduction, detect deterioration, summarize evidence, and recommend attention. They cannot accept residual systemic risk, certify sustainability, close an intervention program, or close a regulatory commitment. Residual-risk acceptance and program closure are immutable human decisions.

## Closure gates
A closure package requires completed implementation, passed independent effectiveness testing, passed sustainability assurance, cross-entity reconciliation, regulatory commitment reconciliation, zero unresolved blockers, and human residual-risk acceptance. Executive closure additionally requires the immutable risk-acceptance and sustainability-assurance versions.

## Post-closure surveillance
Recurrence, material control-health decay, or renewed regulator follow-up create a reopen candidate only. Reopening remains a governed human decision and never occurs automatically.
