# Enterprise Reauthorized Recovery Execution, Systemic Control Re-Transformation and Independent Effectiveness Assurance

This capability begins only after the reopened supervisory recovery investigation records an immutable **Release 95 human enterprise recovery reauthorization**. An AI recommendation, strategy candidate, reopening signal or investigation analysis is not execution authority.

## Enterprise recovery execution

Authorized recovery work is decomposed into evidence-bound enterprise workstreams. Each workstream is linked to Release 95 reauthorization scope and can span multiple entities. Systemic controls with repeated failures may be replaced, redesigned or re-transformed, but control approval remains a human governance decision and is recorded as a separate immutable version.

## Cross-entity sequencing and critical path

Deployment steps are checked for sequence collisions, dependency gaps, blocked milestones, stale evidence, missing human sequencing approval and missing Release 95 scope references. Critical-path governance surfaces cross-entity concentration risk without silently changing execution order.

## Regulatory commitment alignment

Commitments are mapped to recovery workstreams and controls. Owner confirmation, supporting evidence and human regulatory-affairs interpretation are required when regulator meaning is ambiguous. The system never represents regulator intent and never closes commitments automatically.

## Independent effectiveness assurance

Independent recovery testing validates Release 95 scope, repeated-failure coverage, cross-entity effectiveness and evidence completeness. Segregation of duties prevents an implementation owner from performing independent assurance. Passing assurance produces evidence for a later human effectiveness conclusion; it is not itself certification.

## Enterprise-wide validation and outcome readiness

Enterprise-wide control validation, systemic recovery KPIs, blocker escalation and drift analysis feed an outcome-review readiness gate. The gate can only indicate readiness for human outcome review. It cannot certify effectiveness, accept residual risk, close commitments or reclose the program.

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers remain analysis/recommendation/monitoring-only. They cannot approve control re-transformation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reclose recovery programs, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability

`Release 95 human enterprise recovery reauthorization -> enterprise reauthorized recovery execution -> systemic control re-transformation -> implementation evidence -> independent effectiveness assurance -> enterprise-wide control validation -> human outcome review`
