# Reopened Reauthorized Recovery Investigation and Supervisory Recovery Reauthorization

This governance layer starts only after a Release 90 human enterprise reopening decision. It reconstructs the complete recovery/reclosure/reopening history, tests prior recertification assumptions, separates persistent and newly emergent root causes, analyzes failed control re-rehabilitation, maps cross-entity causality, and assesses documented regulator follow-up impact.

## Authority model

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers are analysis/recommendation/monitoring-only. They may reconstruct evidence, score repeated-failure patterns, identify root-cause candidates and prepare renewed strategy options. They cannot authorize recovery remediation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close commitments, reclose the program, represent regulator intent, mutate accounting records or exercise payment/funds authority.

## Human gates

An authoritative investigation requires the immutable Release 90 reopening version plus surveillance and evidence references. Root-cause conclusions remain human-confirmed. A renewed recovery strategy must receive independent internal-audit challenge. Executive reauthorization requires every deterministic readiness gate to be complete and preserves the investigation, conclusion, strategy and challenge references in the immutable authorization version.

## Traceability

`Release 90 human reopening -> reopened recovery investigation -> multi-cycle evidence reconstruction -> repeated-failure root-cause reconstruction -> human conclusion -> renewed recovery strategy -> independent challenge -> executive recovery reauthorization`
