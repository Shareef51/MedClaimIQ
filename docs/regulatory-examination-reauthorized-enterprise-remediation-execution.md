# Reauthorized Enterprise Remediation Execution, Systemic Control Re-Transformation & Independent Recovery Effectiveness Assurance

This capability begins only after an immutable **Release 99 human enterprise remediation reauthorization** with decision `authorize`. A surveillance signal, investigation analysis, AI recommendation, systemic-failure score, or strategy candidate is never execution authority.

## Root-cause treatment and enterprise workstreams
Persistent and emergent systemic root causes confirmed in Release 99 are mapped to human-owned remediation workstreams, controls, entities, evidence, and Release 99 authorization scope. Missing ownership, evidence, scope or control mapping blocks treatment completeness.

## Systemic control re-transformation
Repeated-failure controls may be replaced, retired/redesigned or re-transformed. Every authoritative transformation requires a separate immutable human approval and links back to both Release 99 authorization scope and the root-cause treatment it implements.

## Cross-entity sequencing and critical path
Deployment sequencing surfaces collisions, unmet dependencies, stale evidence, blocked milestones and missing human approvals. The system never silently changes execution order.

## Independent recovery effectiveness assurance
Independent reviewers validate Release 99 scope, systemic root-cause treatment, repeated-failure coverage, cross-entity effectiveness and evidence completeness. Segregation of duties prevents the implementation owner from serving as the independent reviewer. Passing tests are evidence for later human certification, not certification itself.

## Authority boundary
AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers can plan, analyze, recommend, detect drift, score KPIs and monitor. They cannot authorize remediation, approve control re-transformation, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close commitments, reclose the program, represent regulator intent, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

## Traceability
`Release 99 human enterprise remediation reauthorization -> reauthorized enterprise remediation execution -> systemic root-cause treatment -> systemic control re-transformation -> implementation evidence -> independent recovery effectiveness assurance -> enterprise-wide control validation -> human outcome review`
