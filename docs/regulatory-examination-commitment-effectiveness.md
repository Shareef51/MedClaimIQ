# Regulatory Examination Commitment Effectiveness & Sustainability Assurance

This capability governs the transition from a Release 68 completed commitment into evidence-backed, independently validated, human-certified closure and then sustained post-commitment monitoring.

## Traceability

`commitment -> milestone -> evidence -> independent effectiveness validation -> human closure certification -> sustainability surveillance -> human reopening decision`

## Closure gates

Closure readiness requires completed milestones, provenance-valid required evidence, independent effectiveness retesting, resolved dependencies, reconciled regulator follow-ups, and cross-entity implementation where applicable. A numeric readiness score is advisory only; it never creates closure authority.

## Sustainability assurance

After human certification, observations track control health, elapsed sustainability window, recurrence signals, and cross-entity effectiveness. AI/workers may create reopen candidates, but only an authorized human can reopen a commitment.

## Governance

AI, RAG, LangGraph agents, MCP tools, and workers may analyze, score, retrieve, compare, monitor, and recommend. They cannot certify commitment completion, close a regulatory obligation, represent regulator intent, reopen a commitment, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds, or move money.

## Supervisory events

- `regulatory.commitment_effectiveness.sustainability_at_risk`
- `regulatory.commitment_effectiveness.recurrence_candidate`

All outputs retain tenant context, immutable version hashes, human-decision provenance, and audit-ready evidence references.
