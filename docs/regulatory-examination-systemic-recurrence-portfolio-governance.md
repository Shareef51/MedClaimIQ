# Regulatory Examination Systemic Recurrence Portfolio Governance

This capability converts repeated post-reclosure recurrence signals into an enterprise supervisory portfolio without delegating regulatory authority to AI or automation.

## Traceability
`repeat recurrence -> systemic pattern -> enterprise risk -> human supervisory intervention -> remediation program -> independent assurance`

## Core controls
- Aggregate recurring commitments across examinations, entities, controls and regulators.
- Detect shared root causes and systemic control-failure clusters.
- Calculate explainable supervisory materiality scores.
- Correlate regulator follow-ups without inferring regulator intent.
- Require authorized humans to open enterprise intervention cases and approve remediation programs.
- Require independent internal-audit challenge for materially elevated cases.
- Preserve immutable intervention and challenge versions using SHA-256 provenance hashes.
- Emit monitoring-only executive alerts suitable for SSE delivery.

## Non-delegable authority
AI, RAG, agents, MCP tools and workers may analyze, cluster, retrieve, summarize and recommend. They cannot declare authoritative regulatory conclusions, approve intervention programs, reopen/close commitments, certify effectiveness, alter accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.
