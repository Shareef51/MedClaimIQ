# Reclosed Supervisory Recovery Surveillance, Multi-Cycle Decay Intelligence and Enterprise Reopening Governance

This capability begins only after the human executive recovery recertification and human sustainability reclosure produced by the supervisory recovery outcome-validation layer. Its purpose is to determine whether the latest reclosed recovery state remains sustainable across repeated recovery cycles without silently converting surveillance signals into regulatory or executive decisions.

## Surveillance and recurrence intelligence

The runtime monitors post-reclosure control re-transformation regression, multi-cycle recovery decay, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity recurrence propagation, deterioration against the Release 93 recertification/reclosure baseline, new examination/finding correlations, regulator follow-up links, and enterprise materiality. Repeated recovery cycles increase supervisory attention because recurring failure after multiple independent assurance and recertification cycles is treated as an enterprise-governance concern rather than an isolated control event.

## Release 93 provenance

Authoritative investigation/reopening workflows are provenance-bound to an immutable `release93_supervisory_sustainability_reclosure_version_id`. A monitoring signal alone is insufficient to establish that an authoritative reclosed state exists.

## Human governance boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may monitor, score, correlate, summarize and prepare candidates. They cannot open authoritative investigations, determine regulator intent, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose the enterprise recovery program, mutate accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

A reopening requires a human supervisory investigation, independent reassessment, comparison to the prior executive recertification/reclosure, validated cross-entity recurrence scope, human validation of examination/finding links, human interpretation of regulator follow-ups, human validation of enterprise materiality, executive review, internal-audit challenge and a renewed recovery-governance candidate. The final reopening decision is human-only and immutable.

## Traceability

`supervisory recovery reclosure -> post-reclosure surveillance -> multi-cycle supervisory recovery decay -> human investigation -> independent reassessment -> executive/internal-audit challenge -> human enterprise reopening -> renewed recovery governance`
