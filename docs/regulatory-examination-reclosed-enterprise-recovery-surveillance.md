# Reclosed Enterprise Recovery Surveillance, Systemic Recovery Decay Intelligence and Enterprise Reopening Governance

This capability begins only after the human residual-systemic-risk reassessment, executive enterprise recovery recertification and human sustainability reclosure produced by the enterprise recovery outcome-validation layer. Its purpose is to determine whether the latest reclosed enterprise recovery state remains sustainable across subsequent operating cycles without silently converting surveillance signals into regulatory, audit or executive decisions.

## Release 97 provenance

Authoritative investigation and reopening workflows are provenance-bound to an immutable `release97_enterprise_sustainability_reclosure_version_id`. A monitoring signal, model score or worker alert alone is never sufficient to establish that an authoritative reclosed state exists or to reopen the enterprise recovery program.

## Systemic recovery-decay intelligence

The runtime monitors multi-cycle systemic control re-transformation regression, enterprise recovery decay, systemic-risk rebound, cross-entity recurrence propagation, evidence staleness, adverse control testing, regulatory commitment breaches, new examination/finding correlations and regulator follow-up links. It compares current deterioration against the Release 97 executive recertification/reclosure baseline and calculates enterprise materiality for supervisory attention.

Repeated failure after multiple human approvals, independent assurance cycles and executive recertifications is treated as a systemic enterprise-governance concern rather than an isolated control event.

## Human governance boundary

AI, agents, RAG, MCP and workers may monitor, score, correlate, summarize and prepare candidates. They cannot open authoritative investigations, determine regulator intent, accept residual systemic risk, certify recovery effectiveness, close regulatory commitments, reopen/reclose the enterprise recovery program, modify accounting records, authorize payments, collect funds or move money.

An enterprise reopening requires a human investigation, evidence-bound independent reassessment with segregation of duties, comparison to the prior executive recertification/reclosure, validated cross-entity recurrence scope, human validation of new examination/finding links, human interpretation of regulator follow-ups, human validation of enterprise materiality, executive review, internal-audit challenge and a renewed enterprise recovery-governance candidate. The final reopening decision is human-only and immutable.

## Traceability

`Release 97 enterprise recovery sustainability reclosure -> post-reclosure surveillance -> systemic recovery decay -> human investigation -> independent reassessment -> executive/internal-audit challenge -> human enterprise reopening -> renewed enterprise recovery governance`
